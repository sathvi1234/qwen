# I'm Attending poster maker: Qwen Hyderabad × Kramer's Community #3

Visitors enter their name, add a photo, and download or share their own "I'm Attending" poster (Sat, Nov 21, 2026 · 9:30 AM–2:00 PM IST · DevX, Hyderabad).

- Vanilla HTML/CSS/JS, no build step, no backend. Photos are processed in the browser only; nothing is uploaded.
- `assets/poster-template.jpg` is the supplied event poster. Name and photo are drawn into the frame and pill using the geometry constants at the top of `script.js` (`CARD`, `PHOTO`, `PILL`).
- Unofficial community tool. It does not register anyone for the event.
